What we collect

When you use a quote form on this site, you send us your name, phone number, ZIP code, and optionally your email address and a message. The form also records which page you sent it from.

How the form works

Forms on this site are delivered by EmailJS, a third-party service that passes your message to Aging Safely Baths by email. EmailJS processes the message in order to deliver it. We do not sell the information you send.

How we use it

Aging Safely Baths uses your details to contact you about your request, prepare a quote and follow up on your project. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we will.

Phone calls

If you give us your phone number, we will use it to call you about your request. Tell us if you would prefer a different way to be contacted.

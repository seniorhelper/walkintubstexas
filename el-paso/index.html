Walk-in tubs for El Paso and Far West Texas

El Paso is closer to Phoenix than to Houston. That distance, and the desert climate, shape how a walk-in tub project works here.

Distance and how projects work here

El Paso sits at the far western tip of Texas, hundreds of miles from the state's other big metros. For many homes here, a supply-only order installed by a licensed local plumber is the most practical route. We provide phone tech support, help you choose the right tub from your measurements, and ship it to you. If coordinated installation can be scheduled for your address, we will tell you.

El Paso homes

Much of El Paso's housing is single-story, on slab foundations, built from the 1950s onward, with stucco or block exteriors. Single-story homes are ideal for aging in place. Many hall baths have standard alcoves that suit compact walk-in tubs, and larger primary baths in newer east and west side neighborhoods have room for a roll-in shower.

Desert climate notes

  • Dry air is hard on some sealants and rubber gaskets over time. Check the tub's door seal and caulk lines a couple of times a year.
  • Cold winter nights mean a heated seat or quick drain can make a walk-in tub more comfortable, even though days are mild.
  • Summer heat makes garage water heaters run warm; in winter they work harder. Check your heater's size before choosing a deep tub.
  • Monsoon storms can bring sudden heavy rain and power outages. A walk-in tub's door, seat and gravity drain work without power; jets and heaters do not.

Permits

The City of El Paso handles building permits inside city limits. Nearby communities such as Socorro, Horizon City and Anthony have their own offices, and unincorporated El Paso County addresses follow county rules. Plumbing must be done by TSBPE-licensed plumbers and electrical work by TDLR-licensed electricians.

Local help for older adults

  • Rio Grande Area Agency on Aging, coordinated by the Rio Grande Council of Governments, serves El Paso and the neighboring Far West Texas counties. Statewide line: 1-800-252-9240.
  • Veterans: the El Paso VA Health Care System serves the many veterans and military retirees connected to Fort Bliss. Ask your VA provider about HISA.
  • 2-1-1 Texas lists local repair and support programs.

Choosing well

For a single-story El Paso home, both a walk-in tub and a roll-in shower are good options. If installers are hard to schedule, a simpler soaking tub with no electrical needs can make a supply-only install easier for a local plumber.

Working with a local plumber

Share the tub's spec sheet and install guide with your plumber before ordering. Confirm the drain side, door direction, fill volume and any electrical needs. Ask how they will remove the old tub and whether any permit is needed for your address. During the install, your plumber can call our tech support line with product questions.

Before you call

Have your ZIP code, alcove measurements, the drain end and your water heater size ready.

Common questions

Can I get a walk-in tub in El Paso?

Yes. Supply-only tubs ship to El Paso for a licensed local plumber to install, with phone tech support from us. Ask about coordinated installation for your address.

Which aging agency serves El Paso?

The Rio Grande Area Agency on Aging. Call 1-800-252-9240 to be connected.

Does the desert climate affect walk-in tubs?

Dry air can age seals and caulk, so inspect them regularly. Cold nights make heated seats and quick drains worth considering.

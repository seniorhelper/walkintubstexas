What happens after you reach out

  1. A specialist calls you to learn about the person bathing, the bathroom and your timeline.
  2. We discuss options for your home and region: walk-in tub, transfer tub or accessible shower, installed or supply-only.
  3. Measurements are confirmed, either at a site visit where installers are available or with you and your contractor.
  4. You get a written quote that lists the tub and each part of the work, so you can compare or submit it to a program.

Helpful details to have ready

  • Your ZIP code
  • Alcove width and which end the drain is on
  • Water heater size and type
  • Whether the bathroom is upstairs
  • Any program you plan to use, such as VA HISA or STAR+PLUS

Not sure about any of these? Call anyway. We will walk you through it. Our FAQ answers common questions.

Rio Grande Valley region

Walk-in tubs for Rio Grande Valley homes

From McAllen and Edinburg to Harlingen and Brownsville, Valley homes face heat, humidity and hurricane season. A safer bathroom should be built for all three.

Valley homes

Site-built homes

Most homes across Hidalgo and Cameron counties are single-story, on slabs. That is good news for aging in place. Hall baths often have standard alcoves that fit compact walk-in tubs or shower conversions.

Manufactured homes and RV parks

The Valley has many manufactured homes and parks, including communities that fill with seasonal "Winter Texans." Manufactured homes can have lighter floor framing, smaller bathrooms and narrow doors. A filled walk-in tub is heavy, so floor support has to be confirmed, and compact tubs or low-threshold showers are often the better fit. Ask about the park's rules for contractors too.

Heat, humidity and storms

  • Humidity encourages mold. Use a vented exhaust fan, keep surround seams sealed, and dry the door gasket after each bath. The EPA suggests keeping indoor humidity under 60 percent.
  • Hurricane season brings heavy rain, flooding and long outages. The tub's door, seat and gravity drain still work without power; jets, heaters and electric water heaters do not. Keep a battery light handy.
  • Heat keeps incoming water warm much of the year, which helps tub fill temperature, but a small water heater can still run short on a deep tub.

Permits

McAllen, Edinburg, Mission, Pharr, Harlingen, Brownsville and other Valley cities each run their own permit offices. Unincorporated county addresses follow county and utility rules. Plumbing and electrical work requires licensed trades statewide.

Local help for older adults

  • Lower Rio Grande Valley Area Agency on Aging, based in Weslaco, serves Cameron, Hidalgo and Willacy counties. Statewide line: 1-800-252-9240.
  • Veterans: the VA Texas Valley Coastal Bend Health Care System has clinics in Harlingen, McAllen and elsewhere. Ask about HISA grants.
  • TDHCA Amy Young Barrier Removal Program may be available through a local administrator for households with a person with a disability and limited income.
  • 2-1-1 Texas for disaster recovery and local programs.

For Winter Texans

If you split the year between the Valley and another state, think about which home needs the safer bathroom more, and who will be there for delivery and installation. A supply-only order can be scheduled around your arrival.

Before you call

Tell us whether the home is site-built or manufactured, the bathroom size, the drain end and the water heater size. If you are a seasonal resident, tell us your arrival and departure dates.

After a storm

If water gets into the bathroom, do not use jets, heated seats or in-line heaters until the wiring has been checked. Photograph any damage for insurance before cleanup.

Common questions

Can a walk-in tub go in a manufactured home?

Sometimes. Floor support for the filled weight, bathroom size and door widths must be checked. Compact tubs or low-threshold showers are often the better fit.

Which aging agency serves the Rio Grande Valley?

The Lower Rio Grande Valley Area Agency on Aging in Weslaco serves Cameron, Hidalgo and Willacy counties.

Where do Valley veterans apply for HISA?

Through the VA Texas Valley Coastal Bend Health Care System, after a VA provider documents the medical need.

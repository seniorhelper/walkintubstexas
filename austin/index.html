Austin-area housing

Central neighborhoods

Hyde Park, Travis Heights, Clarksville and other central neighborhoods have bungalows and cottages, many on pier-and-beam foundations. Small bathrooms with narrow doors are common. A compact walk-in tub or a shower conversion often fits, and crawl-space access can help with drains.

Suburbs from the 1970s onward

Round Rock, Pflugerville, Cedar Park, Leander, Kyle and Buda have large numbers of slab homes, many two stories. We confirm the stair path and floor framing for upstairs baths, or suggest converting a first-floor bath.

Active-adult communities

Georgetown and other Central Texas towns have large age-restricted communities with single-story homes designed for retirees. These homes often have roomy primary baths that can take a walk-in tub or a roll-in shower easily. HOA rules usually concern exteriors, not bathroom remodels, but contractor access hours may apply.

Water, soil and weather

Hard water is common across Central Texas, so expect to clean scale from seals and jets and consider a softener if buildup is already a problem. East of I-35, clay soils move with wet and dry seasons; west of it, homes often sit on rocky limestone. The February 2021 freeze hit the region hard, so bathroom plumbing on exterior walls deserves a look at insulation.

Permits

The City of Austin's development services department handles permits inside city limits, and Round Rock, Georgetown, Cedar Park, San Marcos and other cities run their own. Unincorporated Travis, Williamson and Hays county addresses follow county and utility rules. Plumbing and electrical work requires licensed trades.

Local help for older adults

  • Capital Area Agency on Aging, coordinated by the Capital Area Council of Governments, serves Bastrop, Blanco, Burnet, Caldwell, Fayette, Hays, Lee, Llano, Travis and Williamson counties.
  • Statewide AAA line: 1-800-252-9240. ADRC: 1-855-937-2372.
  • Veterans: the Central Texas Veterans Health Care System, based in Temple, provides outpatient care in Austin. Ask its Prosthetics office about HISA grants.

Choosing for an Austin home

If the person loves a warm soak and can manage a small step, a walk-in tub with a heated seat is a comfortable choice for Central Texas winters. If mobility is changing quickly, a roll-in shower may save a second remodel. See tub or shower.

Before you call

Note the city your address is actually in, the foundation type, the bathroom floor, and your water heater size. If you live in an active-adult community, check whether it limits contractor hours or parking.

Common questions

Which aging agency serves Austin?

The Capital Area Agency on Aging serves Travis, Williamson, Hays and seven other counties. Call 1-800-252-9240 to be connected.

Where do Austin veterans go for HISA?

The Central Texas Veterans Health Care System, which provides outpatient care in Austin; ask your VA provider and the Prosthetics office.

Do you serve Georgetown and Round Rock?

Supply-only tubs ship anywhere in Central Texas. Call 1-888-779-2284 to ask about coordinated installation at your address.
